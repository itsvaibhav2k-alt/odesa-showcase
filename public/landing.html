You ask — we answer.

Reasonable questions, straight answers.

What happens in an emergency?
Active water leak, no heat in winter, fire or smoke, gas smell, lockout, sewage backup — all classified as emergencies. Alex dispatches a vendor and calls you in parallel. You are never replaced by the AI in an emergency — you're notified alongside the dispatch, every time.
Will tenants know they're talking to an AI?
Alex doesn't lead with "I'm an AI," but answers honestly if asked: "I'm an assistant working for [your landlord]. I can help with most things — for anything I can't, I'll get them on the line." The goal is honesty without theatre.
How does the intent whitelist work?
Alex is fully autonomous for rent balance, office info, emergency detection, and scheduling callbacks. Everything else — payment plans, lease questions, maintenance nuance — runs in shadow mode first: Alex drafts, your team reviews. An intent graduates to autonomous after 20+ clean examples and two sign-offs. Never autonomous: evictions, lease disputes, legal threats, other tenants' information.
What's the difference between Pro and Managed?
Pro is software: you own the dashboard, review the queue, and handle the occasional escalation. Managed is software + a human ops layer: our team reviews every AI decision, handles escalations, and runs your exceptions. Managed costs 4.5% of collected rent; most PM companies charge 8-10%.
Which messaging stack do you use?
Voice runs on Retell over Twilio. SMS runs primarily over Linq with Twilio as a zero-code-change fallback — so if Linq ever goes down, your tenant comms don't. Every message is stored in your dashboard, regardless of which path it took.
How is my tenants' data secured?
AES-256 at rest and in transit. Supabase row-level security for per-organization isolation. Two-party consent recording disclosures in every applicable state. SOC 2 Type I is on the 2026 roadmap. Happy to share our security whitepaper under NDA.
Do I need to rip out Stessa / Baselane / my current tools?
No. Odesa owns tenant operations — calls, texts, maintenance, rent-cycle communication. If your accounting lives in Stessa, keep it there. Pro tier can originate rent collection end-to-end via Stripe + Plaid if you want to consolidate; otherwise we watch and report.
